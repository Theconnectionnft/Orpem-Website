Selected EU Client Work

A selection of projects — from identity and digital builds to campaigns and network-facilitated partnerships.

Branding & Product ↗

Flemish Club Brussels NGO

We led a brand redesign for the Flemish Club Brussels to give the organisation a more modern, contemporary identity, paired with a full website overhaul. Beyond the rebrand, we built the organisation a proprietary event management system — a purpose-built digital tool that lets the team plan, organise, and run its events from a single platform.

Scope — Brand identity · Website · Event platform
Location — Brussels, Belgium
Network Access & Strategy ↗

Salon Liberté

After Salon Liberté's previous sponsor withdrew its support following a shift in the political landscape of its home country, ORPEM stepped in to secure the organisation's future. We connected the organisation with a new investor and guided it through an organisational pivot — repositioning its mission and operating model to fit its new partnership and ensure long-term stability.

Scope — Investor matchmaking · Organisational strategy
Location — Brussels, Belgium
Branding & Growth ↗

EU Jobs Co.

We carried out a full rebrand of EU Jobs Co., followed by a growth strategy built around LinkedIn. Through a structured content and outreach programme, we grew the company's LinkedIn following to over 2,000 and turned the platform into its primary acquisition channel — generating roughly 80% of all company leads. The momentum contributed to the company securing venture capital investment in San Francisco.

80%of leads via LinkedIn

Branding & Negotiation ↗

KUL BRUXMUN

We led a full brand overhaul and Instagram-led marketing strategy for KULBRUXMUN, the KU Leuven Brussels Model United Nations programme. Alongside the rebrand, we represented the organisation in stakeholder negotiations with STUVO's junior enterprise fund — securing a reclassification of its operations that resulted in a significant increase in its financial subsidy.

Scope — Full brand overhaul · Web · Stakeholder outreach
Location — Leuven / Brussels, Belgium

About the Studio

ORPEM is a young creative studio in Brussels, built around one thing: branding that lands inside the EU bubble. We've branded for the sector before — for institutions, companies, and political groups — so we know how this world reads, what it trusts, and what it ignores.

We bring fresh eyes and sharp execution to a space that takes itself seriously — building brands with credibility, marketing with precision, and communications that land, whether our clients are entering the EU landscape for the first time or deepening an existing presence.
