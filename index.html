◎About ORPEM

About

A consortium built for organisations that need to matter in Brussels — and beyond.

ORPEM is a Brussels-based communications and public affairs agency built on a consortium of companies, NGOs, and independent consultants. Our collective expertise spans corporate branding, EU advocacy, government relations across Belgium, Indonesia, and Slovakia, and the EU political startup ecosystem.

We combine creative, strategic, and political knowledge to help our clients build credibility, communicate effectively, and access the right networks — whether they are entering the EU landscape for the first time or deepening an existing presence.
